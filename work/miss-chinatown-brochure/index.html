Print Design

Miss Los Angeles Chinatown Brochure

A heritage fundraiser trifold for the Miss Los Angeles Chinatown Court 2026—A Journey Back to Our Roots. Designed as a keepsake guests can hold in hand, and built here so you can open the cover, turn it over, and read every panel.

Open the cover to step inside—then drag or flip to turn it over.

Designed to unfold

The outside carries the story guests need before they sit down—pageant history, mission, sponsor placeholders, ticket pricing, and a front cover built around the 2026 Court in traditional red qipaos.

Open it and the evening opens with them: a narrative about returning to China, a timed program for “An Evening in China,” and introductions for the Queen and Princesses. Soft pink, florals, and glitter hold the piece together as one keepsake rather than three disconnected panels.

  • Client Miss Los Angeles Chinatown / Chinese Chamber of Commerce of Los Angeles
  • Format Trifold brochure · outside & inside spreads
  • Event Heritage Fundraiser Gala · August 28, 2026
